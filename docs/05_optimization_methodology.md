# Inventory decision policy

The daily heuristic allocates integer transfer units by source, destination and SKU and integer order units by destination and SKU. It approximates incremental expected cost using risk-weighted avoided lost contribution, freight, incremental purchase fees and carrying cost. Transfer benefit per unit is risk × penalty × (1 − promised transit days / 14), less freight and seven days of holding cost. Purchase priority uses half the risk-weighted penalty less holding cost and the purchase fee. These are ranking surrogates. The replay objective measures actual simulated losses and recurring expenses.

Eligible transfers have a positive marginal estimate, a local shortage against lead plus five days of observed demand, a donor with more than nine days of stock, and destination room after committed arrivals. The engine ranks candidates by unit benefit and updates shared donor, receiver and SKU shortage limits as it allocates. Purchases follow a risk-adjusted order-up-to level tied to supplier promise and observed variation. Each line has a cap of 120 units. One supplier serves each SKU. Orders can be released today when position crosses the trigger, otherwise the policy reevaluates tomorrow. The donor reserve is a service guardrail. There is no contractual fill-rate guarantee.

Transfers and orders use the same operational ledger as the comparator. Dispatch, receiving, reservation and orders are reconciled in replay. Greedy ranking runs daily and is inspectable, although it can miss global or multi-period improvements. It assumes unit space and uses conservative reservations. Procurement spend and terminal inventory are reported or discussed separately from recurring decision cost.
